Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

The short version. We collect as little as possible: a family nickname, your kids' first names or nicknames, and your chores and points. We never ask for last names, addresses, birthdays, ages, schools, or photos. There are no ads, no tracking, and no selling of data. You can delete everything at any time.

Kids and parents

The Chore Auction is set up and controlled by a parent or guardian. A parent creates the family, chooses what information to enter, sets up each kid's device, and approves chores and rewards with a parent PIN. Kids use the app to pick chores and mark them finished, under their parent's supervision.

We do not ask kids for any information, and we do not contact kids. We only keep the first names or nicknames a parent enters. A parent can review this information in the app at any time and can delete it completely from Parent, then Family settings, then "Delete everything." A parent can also email us to ask what we hold or to have it deleted.

What stays on your device

Your browser saves your family's private link, your kid's device key, and (only in the demo) the demo family. While a parent is unlocked, a short-lived parent session is saved until the browser tab is closed. We do not use cookies, advertising IDs, or analytics.

The try-it demo runs completely in your browser. Nothing you do in the demo is sent to us.

Where your information is kept

Each family's information is kept in its own private Google Sheet in our Google Drive. It is not shared with anyone. The app's backend runs on Google Apps Script. The app's web page is hosted on GitHub Pages.

These companies act as our service providers:

  • Google stores the family sheets, runs the backend, and sends our emails.
  • GitHub hosts the app's web page. Like any website host, GitHub may log basic visit information such as IP addresses. Your family's private ID is never sent to GitHub.
  • Gumroad processes purchases and issues license keys, under its own privacy policy.

As the app's operator, we can view family information when needed to provide support or keep the service running. We do not look at it for any other reason.

What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your information.
  • We never show ads or share information with advertisers.
  • We never use your information to train AI or build profiles.
  • We only email the parent email you give us, and only about your Chore Auction.

How we protect it

  • Every family has its own private link, made from a long random code.
  • Only a device a parent has set up can pick chores, and only as one kid.
  • Parent actions need the parent PIN. It is stored scrambled, checked on our server, and locks after repeated wrong tries.
  • Parents are emailed about PIN lockouts, PIN changes, and new devices.
  • The app page loads no outside scripts or trackers.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep your family link inside your household, choose a PIN your kids cannot guess, and remove devices you no longer use.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your family's information until you delete your family. When you delete it in the app, the family's sheet goes to the trash and is permanently erased within 30 days. Your nickname and email are removed from our records right away. We keep only the license key, if there is one, so we can still match a refund or support question to a purchase.

You can also email us to delete your family or to ask for a copy of what we hold.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is important, we will tell families with a parent email on file before it takes effect.
