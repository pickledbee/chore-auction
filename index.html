The Chore Auction

Kids bid for chores. The lowest bid wins the job and earns those points when a parent checks the work. Less negotiating, clearer expectations, and kids who chose their own jobs.

Built to be fair

A work limit counts the size of each job, so no one wins everything by bidding 1 and no one gets stuck with all the big jobs.
Every chore has a lowest pay that you set, so no chore is done for nothing.
Skipping the auction never pays more. Leftover chores go to the kid with the least work, at the lowest pay.
Chores rotate, so the same kid is not stuck with the same job every week.
You can limit a chore to the kids who are old enough for it.
Every point earned or spent is listed, so kids can see how it adds up.

Rewards and screen time

You choose the rewards and what they cost. The app keeps track of points and what each kid buys. You hand out the reward yourself. The app does not lock, unlock, or time any devices.

Privacy and safety, in plain words

  • The app never asks for last names, addresses, birthdays, or photos. Your family's information (a family nickname, kids' first names, chores, and points) is kept in its own Google Sheet in the app owner's private Google Drive. It is not shared or sold. The app owner can see it in order to give support.
  • Your family link lets a device view your family's chores and points. Treat it like a house key and only share it within your household.
  • Only devices a parent sets up can bid. On a family computer, each kid has their own secret code.
  • Parent actions need your PIN, which is stored scrambled and checked on the server. It locks after repeated wrong tries, and you get an email if that happens.
  • You can delete your family and all of its data at any time.
